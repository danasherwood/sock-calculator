SockCrafter

Custom Sock Pattern Calculator

Get Started

How it works

  • Make a gauge swatch with your yarn of choice.
  • Measure your stitch and row gauge.
  • Measure your foot.
  • Enter your measurements into the calculator.
  • Generate your custom sock knitting pattern.
  • Copy the instructions into your favourite notes app and start knitting!
